Privacy

This site uses Google Analytics to measure how many people visit and which pages they read. That's the whole reason: aggregate traffic, nothing more.

Consent first

No analytics cookies are set until you accept them in the banner. We run Google Consent Mode with every storage type denied by default, and advertising signals stay denied even after you accept. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, analytics never loads at all. Your choice is remembered locally in your browser; clear your site data to be asked again.

What's collected

When you accept, Google Analytics records standard usage data — pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, and referral source. We don't sell it, and we don't use it to identify you personally.
